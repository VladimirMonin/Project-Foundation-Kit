# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Five cooperating skills for bootstrap, planning, knowledge maintenance, quality and guides.
- Adaptable foundations, working instructions, document and guide templates, and optional Obsidian views.
- Explicit-read and project-scoped integration guidance; no automatic merger or required global setup.
- Read-only structural checker with a pinned PyYAML dependency, unit tests and two synthetic application inputs.
- Public distribution under the MIT license. Private development reports and original commit metadata are not included in the public snapshot.

See [verification boundaries](docs/ACCEPTANCE.md). Structural checks do not establish agent behavior or compatibility with every client.
